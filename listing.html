Why isn't my endpoint in the Bazaar?

Paste your paid endpoint. This runs Coinbase's own validator (the checks the Bazaar indexer runs), looks your payout address up in Coinbase's merchant list, and adds the board's 30-day availability against Coinbase's 99% bar for featured listings. Every problem comes with a fix. Free, nothing is paid.

Same check from an agent: the check_listing tool on the MCP server https://api.settlewatch.com/mcp, or GET https://api.settlewatch.com/v1/listing?url=…. 30 checks a day per address.

How a route gets into the Bazaar

  1. It answers an unpaid request with 402, with the terms in the PAYMENT-REQUIRED header (x402 v2). Input validation that returns 400 before the payment middleware stops the indexer.
  2. It advertises an extensions.bazaar block: an example input that still gets a 402, and the schema that example validates against.
  3. A paid call settles through the CDP facilitator with paymentPayload.resource and paymentPayload.extensions.bazaar set. Indexing can take up to 15 minutes; processing isn't an error, and a resource with no settled payment for 30 days drops out.
  4. The Agentic.Market card (name, category, description) is curated by Coinbase: ask for it with an issue on coinbase/cdp-sdk.
  5. Featured placement needs 99% availability over 30 days on Coinbase's probe, complete metadata and live mainnet payments.
